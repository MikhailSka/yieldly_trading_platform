# Yieldly - Business Description

## Overview

**Yieldly** is an invite-only algorithmic trading development platform designed for retail traders and trading enthusiasts who want to build, test, and refine their trading strategies before risking capital in live markets. Built as a quality-focused solo developer project, Yieldly prioritizes safety, data integrity, and clarity over speed-to-market. Phase 1 delivers a comprehensive backtesting and strategy development toolkit, connecting to major exchanges (Bybit and Binance) in read-only mode to pull market data and portfolio information while keeping users' funds completely isolated. The platform offers both Python-based code editors and visual drag-and-drop builders, enabling traders of any skill level to create sophisticated strategies with built-in indicators and risk management rules. With real-time market data feeds, historical backtesting against actual market conditions, and detailed performance analytics, Yieldly users can validate their trading ideas systematically before committing real capital. The name reflects the platform's core mission: helping traders generate consistent yields through methodical strategy development and rigorous testing.

## Core Benefits

- **Risk-Free Strategy Development**: Build and test trading algorithms using real historical market data without exposing your capital—Yieldly's Phase 1 focuses exclusively on backtesting, ensuring complete safety while you perfect your approach and generate reliable yield projections.

- **Professional-Grade Backtesting Engine**: Simulate your strategies against years of historical data with realistic fee modeling, slippage calculations, and comprehensive performance metrics including win rate, maximum drawdown, profit factor, and equity curves that show true yield potential.

- **Multi-Exchange Portfolio Intelligence**: Connect your Bybit and Binance accounts via read-only API keys to access real-time market data, track portfolio holdings, and monitor P&L across exchanges—Yieldly keeps your trading credentials secure and your funds completely untouched.

- **Dual-Path Strategy Creation**: Build strategies your way—use the Python-based code editor with syntax highlighting, built-in technical indicators (SMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands), and starter templates for full control, or leverage the visual drag-and-drop builder to assemble strategies without writing code. Both paths support sophisticated entry/exit rules, position sizing logic, and risk management—comprehensive documentation and examples ensure traders of all skill levels can develop yield-focused strategies confidently.

- **Unified Dashboard for Yield Tracking**: Monitor real-time portfolio value, asset allocation, transaction history, and basic risk metrics (drawdown, volatility) across connected exchanges in one place—understand your current yields and project future performance with confidence.

- **Invite-Only Beta Access**: Join a curated community of early testers with personalized onboarding based on trading experience level and direct influence on Yieldly's evolution as the platform grows.

## Positioning Statement

*Yieldly: A safety-first algorithmic trading laboratory for retail traders, providing institutional-quality backtesting and strategy development tools without the complexity or capital risk of traditional trading platforms—where ideas become validated, yield-generating strategies before a single dollar is traded.*
